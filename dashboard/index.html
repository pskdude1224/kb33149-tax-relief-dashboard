See how it affects your home.

Florida Amendment 3 (Nov 3, 2026) raises the homestead exemption for non-school local taxes to $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028+. This independent tool models — parcel by parcel across 29 South Miami-Dade ZIP codes, from Downtown Miami and the Rickenbacker corridor through Coconut Grove, Coral Gables, Kendall and Palmetto Bay down to Homestead — exactly what that would do to your street's local tax base and to your own home.

Who represents you — and how to weigh in

Amendment 3 is on your ballot November 3, 2026. Florida legislators shape and defend the amendment; your state Senator and state Representative are the people to call, email, or write. Their positions may shift as they hear from constituents in the districts your ZIP covers.
Find your State Senator
40 senators statewide. South Miami-Dade is split across ~5 districts. Your senator votes on tax and homestead legislation directly.
